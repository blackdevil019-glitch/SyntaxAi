Ideal School of Engineering

IDEAL SCHOOL OF ENGINEERING IS ONE OF THE PREMIER TECHNICAL INSTITUTE OFFERING DIPLOMA IN ENGINEERING SINCE 2008 UNDER S.B.P. TRUST. IT STARTED WITH FOUR BRANCHES OF DIPLOMA IN ENGINEERING COURSE NAMELY, CIVIL ENGG., ELECTRICAL ENGG., ELECTRONICS & TELECOMMUNICATION ENGG. & MECHANICAL ENGG. FROM THE YEAR 2023, ANOTHER BRANCH NAMELY, COMPUTER SCIENCE & ENGINEERING HAS BEEN INCLUDED INTO THE EXISTING DIPLOIMA IN ENGINEERING COURSES.


IT AIMS AT BUILDING THE BUDDING AND ENERGETIC GENERATION WITH HIGHLY QUALIFIED IDEAS AND KNOWLEDGE TO MEET THE PRESENT GOAL AND SCENARIO OF THE COMPANIES. IDEAL SCHOOL OF ENGINEERING IS THE MAN MAKING INDUSTRY THAT NOT ONLY FOCUSES ON KNOWLEDGE OF THE STUDENTS BUT ALSO ON THE MORAL VALUES AND CHARACTER ITSELF.


THE WHEELS OF THE CHARIOT OF THE INSTITUTE RUN SMOOTHLY WITH A GALAXY OF VERSATILE FACULTY MEMBERS, TEACHING AND NON TEACHING STAFFS, AND ALSO THE SUPPORTING STAFFS. ALL THE STAFF MEMBERS ARE ALWAYS COMMITTED TO ACCOMPLISH THE DUTY THEY ARE ASSIGNED WITH MUCH DEDICATION AND ENTHUSIASM.


THE INSTITUTE SHINES BRIGHTLY IN POLLUTION FREE ENVIRONMENT AND IS LOCATED 15KMS FROM BHUBANESWAR (THE CITY OF TEMPLES) ADJACENT TO RETANG RAILWAY STATION, RETANG, BHUBANESWAR.


THE SPRAWLING CAMPUS SPREAD OVER AN AREA OF 52 ACRES SET IN SYLVAN SURROUNDINGS WHICH PROVIDES AN IDEAL AMBIANCE TO STIMULATE THE TEACHING-LEARNING PROCESS, HELPS IN BRINGING OUT SKILLED AND DISCIPLINED DIPLOMA ENGINEERS
